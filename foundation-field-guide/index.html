Field Guide

Foundation

Awareness absorbed in experience.

Primary driver
Stabilization.
Core experience
“How do I make this okay?”
You’re here if
You feel responsible for managing outcomes. You believe effort will equal safety. You improve and don’t feel any safer. You over-function, caretake, explain yourself, and look for reassurance.
Body signals
Contracted and vigilant. Breath shallow, high in the chest.
Thought patterns
Urgent and repetitive. Scanning for what might go wrong. Planning harder under stress.
Emotional quality
Immersive. Sometimes urgent. Sometimes busy, fun, even joyful.
Emotions as guidance
Feelings read as identity. “I feel fear” turns into “I am failing.”
Not to be confused with
The only reality. From inside, this room feels like the whole house. It is one room of it.
Where you may have touched it
The rush of an ordinary day: prepare, respond, manage, repeat.
What tends to emerge
Urgent decisions. Relationships organized around relief. Patterns that repeat in different forms.
The illusion
“If I fix enough, I’ll finally relax.”
The ceiling
You can improve skills here. Relief is fleeting.
What becomes possible
Noticing where you are standing, and staying with the experience before trying to fix it.
The move
Turn toward what is happening in you and ask, “How can I be with you here?”
